An independent publishing house, built on discipline.

Sibani Publishers is the institutional home for work that begins as a personal discipline and becomes a public tool. Every title carries the same standard: accurate, useful, and built to last longer than a trend.

Founded and led by Melokuhle Nsizwa Mhlongo, a Grade 11 Commerce student and Speaker of the Student Parliament at Phendukani Full Service High School in Osizweni, Newcastle, Sibani exists to prove that financial literacy education can be built by the generation it serves, not delivered to them.
