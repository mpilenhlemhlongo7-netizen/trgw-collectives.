## Video direction

- **Palette system** (from `frame.md`, remixed from the real Think Rich, Grow Wise cover): `ink` navy `#0C1B32` — full-bleed grounds, primary text-on-cream. `cream` `#FAF8F2` — card/title fields. `orange` role = lime `#A6CE39` — primary accent (rings, hero numbers, odd-numbered pockets). `yellow` role = orange `#CE712C` — secondary accent (captions, even-numbered pockets, supporting marks). Never invent a new hue.
- **Motion grammar + reveal model:** long-tail `power3` eases throughout, no bounce. Every frame reveals paced to its VO cue — nothing on screen before the line names it. Holds stay still: subtle jitter at most, no lazy breathing, no back-half drift.
- **Rhythm / held-frame allocation:** Frame 2 (system name) and Frame 8 (recap) are the two deliberate breather/held beats — calm before the pocket run, calm before the CTA. Frames 3–7 share one consistent "stage" (a full-width strip, a count-up ring + label) and one consistent `push-slide UP` transition, so the five-item list reads as one flow, not five separate templates — this repetition is intentional (the list's stage), not the banned front-load failure.
- **Negative list:** no stock bokeh / purple-blue "AI" gradient cliché, no soft drop shadows (the pack's hard-offset shadows only), no browser chrome or app UI (this is not an interface), no front-load-then-freeze, no independently-floating screensaver elements.
