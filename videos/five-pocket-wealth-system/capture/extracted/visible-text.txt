Topic: The Five Pocket Wealth System

The Five Pocket Wealth System is the core financial framework from Melokuhle Nsizwa Mhlongo's book Think Rich, Grow Wise, published by Sibani Publishers. It is the flagship teaching tool of TRGW (the Money & Mindset Movement) and Melokuhle's own personal financial operating model.

The system splits every rand earned into five pockets, each with a fixed percentage:

1. Owner's Equity — 15%
2. Learn & Grow — 20%
3. Petty Cash — 15%
4. Reinvestment — 25%
5. Emergency Fund — 25%

Audience: young South Africans, especially students and first-time earners, discovering practical financial literacy for the first time. Delivered in the TRGW voice: authentic, reflective, educational, personal — the reader/viewer is guided, not lectured.
