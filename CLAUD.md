## Project Overview

**trgw-collectives** is the digital platform for the **Think Rich, Grow Wise** movement — financial literacy education rooted in the Five Pocket Wealth System.

The platform is intended to host:

- The book catalogue across both editions
- The Money & Mindset Movement's school and community programmes
- The **@trgwcollectives** content hub spanning TikTok, YouTube, and X
